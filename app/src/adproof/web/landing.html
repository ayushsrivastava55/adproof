Retrieval, measurement and judgement stay separate.

Each layer does only what it can do reliably, and hands the rest on.

  1. Define

    Each requirement becomes a rule with an explicit threshold and a policy for what a "no match" is allowed to conclude. A named person confirms the set, and it is immutable from then on.

  2. Retrieve

    The video is indexed for speech, plus one narrow visual index per visual requirement. Every rule searches for supporting and conflicting moments, so both are equally easy to find.

  3. Measure

    Durations, counts and thresholds are computed in code, never by a language model. Overlapping moments merge once, so nothing is counted twice.

  4. Decide

    A reviewer opens any finding at its exact timestamp, confirms or overrides it with a recorded reason, and signs off. Approval stays unavailable until every requirement is resolved.

Restraint

What AdProof refuses to do.

A verification tool is only worth having if you can trust it when it says it is not sure.

A search miss is not proof of absence

When nothing is found, AdProof records why: likely absent, low confidence, index incomplete, or provider failure. Only the strongest of those can produce a failure, and only when the rule opts in.

Uncertainty stays visible

If a measurement lands within the sampling resolution of its threshold, it goes to a person instead of picking a side.

Machine and human stay distinct

Automated results are immutable. An override sits beside the original, never on top of it, with a recorded reason.

No fabricated results

If the analysis service fails you get a visible error, not a plausible looking report. There is no fixture data anywhere in the product, and no silent fallback.

Subjective requirements such as "feels premium" are routed to human review and never machine scored, by design rather than by omission.

Questions

Does AdProof approve or reject on its own?

No. It produces a recommendation with evidence, and your policy decides whether a person must sign off. It will never auto-reject on the strength of something it simply failed to find.

How accurate is the visual duration measurement?

Visual analysis samples a frame at a fixed interval, so measurements are accurate to roughly that interval, and that figure is shown next to every result. Anything falling within one interval of the threshold goes to a person instead of being guessed.

Who can see our creators' unpublished videos?

Only members of your workspace. Playback is proxied through AdProof behind a short-lived token bound to one person and one asset, and the underlying storage URL is never sent to a browser.

Can we see how a result was produced?

Every piece of evidence carries the query that retrieved it, the index it came from, the rule version applied and the raw provider response. Reports are versioned and history is append-only.

Is this a chat-with-video tool?

No, deliberately. The question it answers is narrow: did this submission satisfy the agreed requirements, and what evidence supports that conclusion?
