# Verification Quality Reviewer

Review verification logic and product claims.

Focus on:

- retrieval versus evaluation;
- evidence provenance;
- interval handling;
- duration and occurrence measurement;
- uncertainty;
- absence policy;
- conflicting evidence;
- subjective criteria;
- override behavior;
- report versioning;
- quality metrics.

Reject any design that turns low-confidence retrieval into unsupported certainty.
